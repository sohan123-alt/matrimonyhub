Privacy Policy

Marriage is personal. Here is exactly what we keep, who sees it, and how you stay in control.

On this pageWhat we collectHow we use itWho can see whatServices we rely onYour choicesKeeping data safeCookies and storageQuestions

What we collect

  • Account: your name, email address and an encrypted password.
  • Profile: age, gender, religion, education, occupation, location, physical details, family details and the photo you upload.
  • Contact: your mobile number, if you add one.
  • Payments: the plan, method, amount and transaction ID you submit. We never see your bKash, Nagad or bank PIN.

How we use it

  • To show your profile to other members and let you browse theirs.
  • To verify profiles and confirm premium payments.
  • To send account emails such as password resets.

We do not sell your personal data.

Who can see what

  • Logged-in members can see your profile details and photo.
  • Your mobile number is hidden from everyone except premium members, and our staff when they verify your profile.
  • Visitors who are not logged in cannot browse profiles.

Services we rely on

We store account and profile data with Supabase and profile photos with Cloudinary. The site is hosted on Vercel. These providers process data only to run the service for us.

Your choices

  • Edit or update your profile whenever you like.
  • Ask us to delete your account and data by emailing support@matrimonyhub.com.
  • Ask for a copy of the data we hold about you.

Keeping data safe

Passwords are hashed and access to data is limited by database security rules. No system is perfectly secure, so please choose a strong, unique password and never share it.

Cookies and storage

We use your browser's local storage to keep you logged in. We do not use advertising cookies.

Questions

Write to support@matrimonyhub.com and we will reply as soon as we can.
