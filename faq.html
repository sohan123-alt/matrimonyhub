Questions families ask us

Quick answers about joining, verification and premium. Cannot find yours? Message us.

Is it free to join?

Yes. Creating an account, building a profile and browsing other profiles cost nothing. Premium is optional and unlocks contact details.

What does the verified badge mean?

A staff member reviewed the details on that profile. It shows that someone looked, but you should still meet and speak with a person, with your family, before deciding.

Why can I not see phone numbers?

Phone numbers are private. Only premium members can see them, and members only see a number if its owner added one.

How do I upgrade to premium?

Open Premium in your dashboard, choose a plan, send the money by bKash, Nagad, Rocket or bank transfer, and paste the transaction ID. We confirm it and switch premium on, usually within a few hours.

My payment is still in review. What now?

Reviews are done by hand, so please allow a few hours. If it takes longer, write to us on the contact page with your transaction ID.

Can I change or hide my profile?

You can edit your profile at any time from My profile. To remove it completely, email us and we will delete your account and data.

I forgot my password.

Use the Forgot password link on the login page. We will email you a link to choose a new one.

Can a family member register for me?

Yes, as long as you know about it and agree. The profile must describe the person who is looking to marry.
