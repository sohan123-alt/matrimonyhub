Who can join

  • You must be 18 or older and legally allowed to marry under the laws that apply to you.
  • You may register only for yourself, or for a family member with their knowledge and consent.
  • One person may hold one account.

Your profile

You promise that everything on your profile is true and about you. Do not use another person's photo or details.

  • Profiles with false information may be removed without notice.
  • A verified badge means our staff reviewed your details. It is not a guarantee about a person's character.

How to behave

You agree not to:

  • harass, threaten or pressure other members
  • ask for money or send unwanted promotional messages
  • share another member's contact details without permission
  • use the service for anything other than finding a marriage partner

Premium membership and payments

  • Premium plans (Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly) unlock contact details and extra features for the plan period.
  • You pay by bKash, Nagad, Rocket or bank transfer and submit the transaction ID. Premium starts after our team confirms the payment.
  • Payments are non-refundable once premium has been activated, unless the law says otherwise.
  • If a transaction cannot be found, we may reject it and tell you why.

Suspension and removal

We may suspend or remove accounts that break these terms. You can ask us to delete your account at any time by writing to support@matrimonyhub.com.

Our responsibility

MatrimonyHub is a platform that introduces members. We cannot promise a match, and we are not responsible for what members say or do. Meet people in safe, public places and involve your family before making decisions.

Changes to these terms

We may update these terms. When we make an important change, we will tell members on the site. Continuing to use MatrimonyHub means you accept the updated terms.
