MatrimonyHub helps families in Bangladesh find a life partner through profiles that real people have reviewed.

Why MatrimonyHub exists

Too many matrimony sites are full of fake photos, half-filled profiles and strangers who are not serious. Families lose weeks sorting through them.

We keep it small and careful. Every profile can be checked by our staff, phone numbers are never public, and you pay in the way you already do every day, with bKash, Nagad, Rocket or a bank transfer.

How verification works

When you create a profile, it goes into a review queue. A member of our staff checks the details you gave and, if everything looks right, adds the verified badge. You can keep using the site while you wait.
